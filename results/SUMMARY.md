## Known limitations

- The vector baseline is the naïve chunk-and-embed implementation, not hybrid search with a reranker. See `docs/DECISIONS.md`.
- The baseline answers single-shot while PageIndex answers agentically. The asymmetry favours PageIndex and is disclosed rather than buried.
- With n≈90 the 95% CI on accuracy is roughly ±7 points. Differences inside the interval are not claimed as wins.
